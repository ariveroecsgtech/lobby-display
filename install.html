Install ECSG Display

Put your building's screen on your phone or computer, like any other app. It takes less than a minute and there is nothing to download from a store.

  1. Open this page in Safari. On an iPhone, apps can only be installed from Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button: the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add at the top right. The app icon appears on your home screen.
  5. Open the app from that icon from now on.
Needs iOS 16.4 or later. Check in Settings, General, About.
  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Tap Install. The app icon appears with your other apps.
On Samsung Internet: tap the menu, then "Add page to", then "Home screen".
  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Look at the right end of the address bar for the install icon: a small screen with an arrow. Click it.
  3. If you do not see it, open the browser menu (three dots) and choose Install ECSG Display, or Apps, then Install this site as an app.
  4. Click Install. The app opens in its own window and is added to your Start menu or Dock.
On a Mac with Safari: File menu, then "Add to Dock".
